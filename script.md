## The deal

[stat: $330M | upfront]
$330 million dollars. Up front.

[stat: $2.3B | in milestones]
That's what Pfizer just paid to license three pre-IND in vivo CAR T programs from Starna Therapeutics... with up to $2.3 billion in milestones behind it.

## The bet

[emphasize: "inside the patient"]
CAR T has always been cancer medicine. Remove the patient's T-cells. Engineer them in a lab. Re-infuse. Expensive, slow, bespoke. Starna's bet is different... engineer the T-cells inside the patient, using RNA.

## The target

[emphasize: "autoimmune disease"]
And the first target isn't cancer. It's autoimmune disease... a market an order of magnitude larger than cancer CAR T.

## The catch

[emphasize: "not a product"]
Three programs. None yet in people. The next real read is an IND filing. Until then... $330 million is the price of a platform option... not a product.
