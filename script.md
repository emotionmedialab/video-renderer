## 7 days of wear

7 days. That's the new wear clock for Tandem's tubeless Mobi insulin pump, just cleared by the FDA.

## Doubled vs Omnipod

Omnipod 5, the incumbent tubeless pump, wears 3 days. Tandem has doubled that on the infusion set.

## The catch

The catch? The cartridge still changes every 3 days. And the US launch waits until the first half of 2027.

## Pharmacy-only

It goes to market pharmacy-only. That reroutes distribution away from the durable equipment channel, which changes the reimbursement path, and the scale mechanics, for a pump launch.

## The open question

One wear interval doubled. One distribution channel reset. Whether Tandem takes pump share from Insulet, or just forces Insulet to move, is the open question.
